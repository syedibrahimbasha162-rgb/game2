Proper Ludo

Classic 4-color Ludo: You (Red) vs 3 CPU players

Turn

Player: Red

Dice: -

Message: Roll to start.

Rules

  • Roll 6 to move a token out of yard.
  • Move around 52-track, then enter your home lane.
  • Exact roll is required to reach center and finish.
  • Landing on opponent sends them back to yard.
  • Safe cells cannot be captured.
  • Three consecutive 6s forfeits your turn.
  • First player to finish all 4 tokens wins.
